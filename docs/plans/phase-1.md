## Definition of done (BTP plan §12, adapted)

- Every BTP rule has a source in its comment and in `docs/research/BTP_RULES.md`, and cases that fire
  and stay silent; a rule without cases fails `pnpm verify`.
- A sous-traitant's invoice with VAT is an error in the pane (sheet invoice and received invoice),
  only once the relationship is marked; a company not marked BTP sees no BTP warning.
- The 50 golden documents give what they must (kind, stage, findings, text found); the same file
  twice makes one document; p95 under 5 s without OCR and 30 s with OCR on this server, measured.
- `docker build` gives an image where `tesseract --list-langs` lists `fra`.

## Deviations

**1A** (2026-10-24):

- The rules' cases are in the existing `fixtures/invalid/btp.json` (each case: the rule, the golden it
  starts from, what it changes, the context) and the golden `26-btp-situation-retenue`, not in a new
  `packages/core/fixtures/btp/rules.json`: `packages/core/src/rules/rules.test.ts` already fails the
  gate for any rule without a failing case or a golden it stays silent on, BTP rules included.
- A sous-traitant's VAT on a received invoice is its own flag `subcontracting_vat` at level warn, not
  one line of the « rules » info: it is money (VAT not deductible, owed again under autoliquidation),
  but not a block, since a sous-traitant selling materials alone charges VAT.
- The supplier's switch is in Réception, on each invoice of a supplier with a SIREN: the pane has no
  supplier list, and that is where the question arises.
- BTP-RG-* and BTP-SIT-* speak only where the caller gives the retenue and the situation; the sheet
  does not yet (`docs/DEFERRED.md` › BTP rules).

**1B** (2026-10-24):

- The golden documents' generator is `apps/api/scripts/golden/documents.ts`, not `scripts/golden/`:
  the package boundaries refuse a test that reaches outside its package, and the generator needs
  `@fi/core`'s Factur-X writer and goldens. It writes the PDFs by hand (Helvetica, WinAnsi) and
  makes the scans and images with `pdftoppm`, so nothing binary is committed.
- No pane screen for `POST /ingest` yet: the pane's « Fichiers importés » (R6) keeps reading
  workbooks and invoices for the registry. Stage 2 gives the documents their screen, once they are
  classified (`docs/DEFERRED.md`).
- A document with text but no structured invoice stays `ingested`, its text in `extracted`, until
  stage 2's classifier: `classified` would claim a kind nobody gave it. The worker reads it once:
  `extracted` set is what « read » means.
- The supplier or customer is found at validate (by SIREN, `party_id`), not at ingest: stage 2's
  linking step proposes « confirmer_tiers » for one not known.
- The drive and e-mail adapters: drive waits for a folder picker (`docs/TODO.md`), e-mail is 1C.
- A web page (`<!DOCTYPE html` or `<html`) is refused at the door: it starts with a tag like XML, and
  an e-mail's body saved as a file would otherwise go to review as broken XML.

**1C** (2026-10-24):

- `apps/api/src/ingest-mail.ts`, not `mail.ts`: `mail.ts` is the outgoing mailer (sign-in links,
  reminders).
- `POST /ingest/mail` takes a raw e-mail as its body (35 MB at most, the size mail servers take with
  the base64): what a forwarding provider will call once the owner picks one (D3), and what a person
  can use today with an e-mail saved as `.eml` from Outlook or Gmail.
- A message forwarded as an attachment is opened, three levels deep at most: the invoice is inside.
  An image the HTML shows by its Content-ID (a logo, a signature) is skipped, as are empty parts,
  parts over 20 MB and anything the worker cannot read (a Word file); the answer lists them with the
  reason. An e-mail with nothing to read is refused (422), nothing stored.
- The backlog is checked once for the whole e-mail, so an e-mail is stored whole or not at all when
  the company is busy. The `sourceRef` of its documents is its Message-ID, or `sha256:` of the e-mail
  when it has none; its Date header is not trusted for `receivedAt` (the time it reached us is).

**1D** (2026-10-24):

- OneDrive only: `drive.file` does not say whether a folder picked in Google's picker grants its
  files (`docs/GOOGLE.md` §2), so Google Drive folders wait for a real account (`docs/TODO.md`).
- Each check lists the folder (Graph `children`, 200 a page, 25 pages: the first 5 000 items) and
  compares each file's content tag with the one last seen, instead of a change feed: `delta` is
  documented on a drive's root only, a whole OneDrive to filter (`docs/MICROSOFT365.md`).
  Subfolders are not walked (`docs/DEFERRED.md`).
- What a folder held is kept per file in `folder_items` (its content tag, the document it gave or
  why it was skipped), not read off `documents`: documents dedupe by their bytes per company, so the
  same invoice in two folders, or put back, is one document, and a skipped file has no document.
- The files already in the folder when it is picked are read too: a person picks the folder where
  their invoices already are. 50 files are read at most per check; a folder with more is marked
  `behind`, and the sweep checks it again at once instead of waiting for the next notification or
  poll (`connectorsBehind`). The company's backlog full (`ingest_busy`) stops the folder for this
  check without `behind`: the next check takes the rest.
- A file gone from the folder is forgotten; put back, it is read again and its bytes give back the
  same document.
- The folder keeps the pane's « Mode test » of the moment it was picked: its documents are test
  documents whatever the pane shows later. Picked again in the other mode, the files read from then
  on follow it.
- 5 folders per connection (`MAX_FOLDERS`), one at a time in the picker.
- The documents a folder gives are audited with the actor `connector:microsoft`; the pick and
  « Ne plus surveiller » with `user`.
- « Ne plus surveiller » and « Déconnecter » delete the folder and what it held (`folder_items`),
  never the documents it gave: they are the company's, read and perhaps already linked.
